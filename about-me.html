About me

I'm Brendan, a Computer Science major and Africana Studies minor at Columbia University. I like hip hop, painting, streetwear, soccer (guess my Premier League team), and of course, photography

I started taking 35 mm film pictures a few years back before switching to digital. I like capturing slices of life: I enjoy finding the charm in imperfection and immortalizing candid moments. Portraits are my favorite type of photo to take though.
